## Claim
The +2.7/100k post-adoption association reflects a causal effect of the policy.

## Courtroom
- Estimand: group-weighted ATT, signed and bounded
- Falsification: placebo distribution; Rambachan-Roth robust CI; control-pool sensitivity

## Kills it
A robust CI that includes zero (R&R), a placebo p > 0.10, or magnitude that halves with control choice.

## Evidence
- [[2026-06-04_not-causal]] — R&R cannot sign it; placebo p=0.25; drop post-communist -> +1.2; loads on 2008.

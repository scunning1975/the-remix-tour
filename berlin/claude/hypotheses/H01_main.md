## Claim
Adopting a national suicide-prevention strategy lowered a country's suicide rate relative to its counterfactual.

## Courtroom
- Estimand: group-size-weighted ATT of strategy adoption on the age-standardised suicide rate
- Population: European countries adopting 1995-2005 (Norway, Sweden, UK, Ireland)
- Variation: staggered timing of national adoption vs documented never-adopters
- Falsification: in-space placebo among never-treated should be null; pre-trends should be flat

## Kills it
A confirmed protective effect would require a negative ATT that survives Rambachan-Roth and the placebo. We find a POSITIVE point estimate that does not survive either — so the protective claim is not earned, and neither is a harm claim. Verdict: cannot identify.

- [[2026-06-04_positive-att]] — estimate is +2.7, not negative
- [[2026-06-04_pretrends-flat]] — PT not rejected under universal base

## Finding
The static TWFE coefficient (3.11) decomposes into 2x2 comparisons whose weighted average
reconstructs it exactly (diff 2.6e-14). The "forbidden" already-treated-as-control comparisons
carry only ~3% of the weight, because the never-treated pool is large. So TWFE (3.11) ≈ CS (2.7):
the staggered-DiD pathology is small here, and the identification problem is shared by all estimators.

## Claim
Under the correct universal (g-1) baseline, treated and control suicide rates were on parallel paths before adoption.

## Courtroom
- Estimand: pre-period event-study coefficients (long differences from g-1)
- Falsification: joint test of pre-coefficients rejects flat trends

## Kills it
A jointly significant pre-trend (e.g., sum z^2 far exceeding the chi-square(8) critical value).

## Evidence
- [[2026-06-04_pretrends-flat]] — universal-base sum z^2 = 9.6 (df 8), does not reject; the varying-base "violation" (59.4) was a short-difference artifact.

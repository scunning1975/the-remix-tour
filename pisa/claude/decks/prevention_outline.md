# Prevention Without Proof — Deck Outline

## Audience & rhetoric
EC / JRC economists (policy-literate, technically sophisticated). Balance: Logos 45 / Pathos 35 / Ethos 20.
One sentence to remember: **"We have been making life-and-death policy in a darkness we mistook for evidence."**

## Theme
azzurri.sty — Italy-blue core, tricolore frame-title tab, four gold World-Cup stars on title, azure full-bleed dividers.

## Arc (≈40 slides; 65 min)
### Act I — Tension (story)
1. Title
2. "Every 40 seconds" (bignumber, pathos)

5. The only question: do they save lives?
6. The answer up front (pyramid): we cannot tell — and that should frighten us

### Act II — Investigation

15. DIVIDER: The design

17. The covariate an expert would insist on: unemployment
18. No overlap: the propensity score separates (fig3) → regression adjustment
19. Sixteen clusters: trust randomization inference, not t-stats
20. DIVIDER: What the data say
21. The estimate is positive: +2.7 more suicides (the surprise)
22. Three estimators agree (fig_robust_overlay / table)
23. A careless reader would stop here
24. DIVIDER: What the number is NOT
25. Pre-trends fail (fig8 event study)
26. The Ashenfelter ascent: strategies are reached for when suicide climbs
27. Caveat: the long leads are UK & Ireland only (honesty)
28. The effect loads on 2008 (fig8c calendar) — the recession
29. Rambachan–Roth: the effect cannot be signed (fig9)
30. In-space placebo: indistinguishable from chance (fig_placebo, p=0.25)
31. Synthetic control, last resort: ordinary gaps (fig_synth UK/IE)
32. Fragile: drop post-communist controls → halves to +1.24
33. DIVIDER: Devil's advocate
34. "You're explaining away an inconvenient result" — the response
35. DIVIDER: What this means
36. Not that strategies fail — that the design cannot reveal their worth

39. Italy never wrote the document — the silent control
40. Closing: the one sentence (full-bleed)

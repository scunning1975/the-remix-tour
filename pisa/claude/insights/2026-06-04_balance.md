## Finding
At baseline, the standardized difference in means between treated and control is **0.33 for
unemployment** — above the Imbens & Rubin (2015) threshold of 0.25, hence "imbalanced". This
justifies conditioning on unemployment. Suicide (-0.99), share 65+ (0.63), and median age (-0.42)
are also imbalanced; only unemployment is used as a time-varying covariate (GDP has 1994 gaps).

## Key Numbers
| Variable | Treated | Control | Std. diff | Imbalanced? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Suicide rate | 12.13 | 21.53 | -0.99 | yes |
| Unemployment | 9.78 | 8.18 | 0.33 | yes |
| Share 65+ | 15.23 | 13.95 | 0.63 | yes |
| Median age | 35.20 | 36.24 | -0.42 | yes |

## Context
Std. diff = (mean_T - mean_C) / sqrt((var_T + var_C)/2). Baseline = first observed year per country.
